Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 26, 2026 | Last Updated: November 2026

1. Overview

OrderFlow Pro ("we", "our", or "the extension") is developed by OrderFlow Studio. We provide browser automation tools to help users export order records and receipts. We operate under a strict policy: your financial, purchase, and personal information belongs strictly to you.

2. Zero Server Data Collection

  • 100% Client-Side Processing: OrderFlow Pro parses order data directly in your browser's local memory. We do not operate cloud servers that collect, transmit, store, or sell your purchase history, item names, prices, or customer identities.
  • No Telemetry or Tracking: We do not inject behavioral trackers, analytics pixels, or advertising identifiers.

3. Permissions Used

  • activeTab & scripting: Used exclusively when you explicitly click the extension popup to extract visible order details from your active Amazon tab.
  • downloads: Required to save the generated CSV spreadsheet and ZIP invoice package to your local Downloads directory.
  • storage: Used solely to remember your Pro license status and UI preferences locally.
  • https://api.creem.io/*: Used solely to validate and activate your lifetime license key with our payment processor (Creem.io).

4. Payment Processing

All commercial transactions are processed through Creem.io (Merchant of Record). We never collect, store, or have access to your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or payment credentials.
