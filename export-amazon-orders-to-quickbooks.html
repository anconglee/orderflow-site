The Problem: Why Amazon and QuickBooks Don't Talk

If you purchase supplies, office equipment, software, or inventory through Amazon for your LLC or sole proprietorship, you know the frustration:

  • Credit card feed only shows vague charges: Your bank feed in QuickBooks displays cryptic charges like AMZN Mktp US*2K7B9 without item descriptions.
  • No bulk export in Amazon: Amazon removed the CSV report generator, forcing users to click into each order individually.
  • Missing receipts for CPA reconciliation: CPAs and tax auditors demand itemized proof for business expense deductions.

Step-by-Step: Exporting Amazon Orders to QuickBooks with OrderFlow Pro

1

Open Amazon "Your Orders" in Chrome or Edge

Navigate to amazon.com/your-orders/orders. Make sure you are logged into the Amazon account holding your business transactions.

2

Filter by Tax Year and Click Export CSV

Click the OrderFlow Pro icon in your browser toolbar. Select your target tax year (e.g. Tax Year 2026 or All Time) and click "Export CSV". OrderFlow will automatically scrape visible orders and download a clean spreadsheet.

3

Import into QuickBooks Online

In QuickBooks Online, go to Accounting ➡️ Transactions ➡️ Banking. Click "Upload transactions" or use the QuickBooks CSV Import tool with this standard column mapping:
